The ordinal policy selects 23 old IDs and four of six immediate families.
It never selects Timber/Treasury in the new pool: other offered IDs sort earlier.
That is policy bias, not evidence those rewards are bad or unavailable. All six
families have separate real authoritative grant/receipt tests. New selected
families and counts: loot-recovery 218, loot-equipment 457, loot-provisions 277, loot-stone 25.
The same supply repeats well beyond the former two-stack cap without exhausting
the W1–19 chooser. All 218 selected recoveries produce positive living healing;
no selected recovery is a no-op. These runs rarely expose surviving Keep damage,
so Keep repair/caps additionally use explicit pure and arithmetic fixtures.

Ordinary recovery on Ready and research progress/purchases are retained in
the raw commands. Lost permanent production/stat/upkeep bonuses change later
affordable actions and casualties; repeatable supplies strengthen some purchasing.
Thus the command streams intentionally diverge after choices. A 49/60
policy Victory count is not a balance conclusion, and this policy is not optimized.
