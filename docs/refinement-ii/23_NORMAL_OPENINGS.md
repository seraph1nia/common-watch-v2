## Removal and consolidation inventory

- Remove numbered1/4–4/4 lessons and home-visit tracking, including the remembered
  boolean and its session/visibility updates. Ready was and remains ungated.
- Remove repeated prior-battle summary from planning guidance. The frozen result
  and immediate reward keep their existing owners; second planning can point at
  another recruit instead of repeating the previous report.
- Remove the duplicate zero-Wood recovery label above the build grid. The bounded
  recovery choice remains in Production only for the relevant shortage.
- Put Lumbermill first, followed by Metal/Food production. Show exact next-Ready
  per-producer output using Core's research/output and schedule functions, beside
  its actual construction cost. Full categories/catalog and numerical doctrine
  disclosure remain available.
- Give trainer guidance its explicit Military destination, replacing the old
  Farm/Lumber heuristic. Navigation selects context and spends nothing.
- Shorten starting doctrine implications; remove routine recovery promotion and
  promises of an immediate specialist. Keep one optional context prompt, its
  collapse preference, and the existing final-Ready/Food consequence.

Merchant's normal route has an extra producer purchase. No claim of fewer actions
for every doctrine is made. Default guide interruptions stay zero; no extra
panel, tutorial lock, automatic purchase or mandatory home inspection is added.
First-time comprehension and enjoyment remain unmeasured.
