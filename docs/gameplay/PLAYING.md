# Playing the complete Godot interface

Commands and detached authoritative snapshots still own prices, soldiers, technologies and phase. The explicitly changed combat topology uses six columns per player, up to six players; damage and unit counts are unchanged.

When combat actually starts, preparation details close and unconfirmed purchases
or retirement are cancelled. Settings, Menu and explicit exit consent remain
usable with the current phase stated; **Return to battle** cancels exit consent
safely. A reward opens after the last playback effect completes. Its accepted
receipt names the chosen card; hover for its effect and observed stock/healing
changes. The chooser leaves only after acceptance, including an immediate solo
return to planning. Teammate waiting permits read-only inspection, without a
next-wave army preview. No extra Continue is required. Details reopen only when
requested; the prior inspected city and clearing return as harmless context.

- **Single Player** opens three distinct random doctrine cards. Select one, then press **Start Match**. Offers stay stable through settings, resizing and reconnection; a new match identity creates new offers. **Multiplayer** holds LAN, reconnect and Steam operations, with personal doctrine selection and host-only start after every connected player chooses. **Settings** preserves audio preferences. **Exit Game** asks for confirmation before normal shutdown.
- Planning shows the inspected city, Keep and six stocks beside signed production income. One short threat summary groups incoming enemies and names participating soldiers; only funding exceptions stay prominent. Hover it for exact Food payment/timing, or use **City details** for the full manifest and completed upkeep. Selecting a clearing yields the summary to its building context; **×** clears selection. **Menu → Settings** opens preferences. Ready/waiting retains the committed ledger and read-only inspection routes, with **Unready** reopening editing. Combat removes the ledger, completed preparation track and building controls; the actual live arena has no redundant Show arena button. Damaged Keep health remains visible. Chosen rewards leave the three-card chooser for a compact accepted-choice/team-waiting view. Fallen/terminal views hide personal management. Fixed settlement framing and all nine clearing targets remain unchanged.
- **Team** stays below Menu in multiplayer: compact Ready/Choosing counts and city buttons, with visible low-Keep, army/Food, Away and Fallen exceptions. Expand its heading for exact required players and inspected-city details, including read-only Army/Worldtree. Click a city to inspect and focus that physical sector; Ready and reward commands still belong to your own city. Manual camera and Overview are labeled; **Focus P#** or **R** restores the inspected sector, and your own city button returns locally. Live inspection separates troop ownership from its actual physical sector. After your Keep falls, current survivors may finish the battle, but later waves exclude your army. The first automatic observation waits for your own survivors, then picks the lowest-health connected standing ally (ID breaks ties). Explicit city/camera choices remain yours through later health updates. **Watch P#** deliberately changes observation. Original enemies are still redistributed among survivors; host lifetime and reconnect requirements remain unchanged. Terminal views offer no future production, recovery or reward.
- Accepted purchases and army assignments get short gold labels near their visible target; rejected commands get a coral response and retain the complete reason in recent feedback. Production outlines the resource ledger. Battle start, boss arrival, clear, defeat and victory have distinct short cues; rapid hits are limited. Music volume zero mutes only the soundtrack, so UI and battle responses remain available. Combat sound onset and pause follow presented combat. See the [feedback review](FEEDBACK_REVIEW.md) for evidence and the native audition.
- Occupied buildings show a small **L#** foundation badge: neutral at L1, gold after upgrades. The same approved painting remains in place; the number reads its actual level without changing its clearing or click target. Secondary disclosures, roster selection and session links use quiet forest surfaces; primary choices/Ready retain emerald emphasis. Reconnect appears when disconnected; feedback history stays in City details; pending, rejected and disconnected notices remain beside match actions.
- The floating dock offers **Build**, **Worldtree**, **Troops & homes** and **City details**. **×** clears a selected plot to expose the map. Select any clearing. Empty owned land offers **Production**, **Military**, **Defense**, **Trade** and **Utility** tabs. Selecting a card spends nothing; confirmation pays the quoted construction cost. Lumbermill's **4 Gold recovery** card also requires confirmation. Locked land shows its purchase quote. Occupied plots provide upgrades, sale, recruitment, trading and relevant management controls.
- **Troops & homes** opens one army workspace. Homes 1–3 form the front row facing the enemy, 4–6 the rear toward the Keep. Select an occupied home to select its squad; select a legal outlined destination, then **Move selected squad**. Recruitment stays beside the board and shows its actual destination and price. A compatible trainer/role follows the selected squad; use the trainer or role choice for alternatives and **Destination** for automatic, new or another compatible squad. **Members** reveals the optional roster; selecting a member opens bounded identity, equipment, service, regroup, store/return and reviewed-retirement details. Storage exposes Town Hall capacity/recovery; Quotes retains complete recruit profiles. Selecting locked land or a tower reveals that home's purchase, build, upgrade/branch and sale actions. All six homes remain visible while secondary detail scrolls. Homes remain separate from nine settlement plots; squads and towers cannot share one. Early funding uses current stock, final preparation includes the final production, and reward waiting shows the last accepted battle rather than a fabricated next-wave plan. Allied homes use the same workspace for read-only inspection.
- **Worldtree** retains all 18 technologies, prerequisites, exclusive branches, costs and acquired effects. Select a node, then purchase. Allied, fallen and disconnected cities remain read-only; inspecting another city never changes authenticated command identity.
- **Ready · prep 2 / prep 3 / battle** names the next transition. All connected, standing cities must be Ready before collecting one production payment and advancing research/eligible recovery. The third settlement also pays Food and starts battle. **Unready** reopens your city for editing while waiting. The readiness count excludes disconnected and fallen cities. Inspecting an ally keeps Ready attached to your own labeled city. Food shortages permit readiness; unfunded field members sit out, while stored members never fight. **Pause/Resume** affects the whole match; only the original host controls combat speed. Local dialogs never pause cooperative play.
- **Show arena** previews the forthcoming field army, enemies and structures during preparation. Click a combatant or structure to inspect it. Combat frames the focused sector's actual engagement and defenses in the available HUD space. Native pan/zoom and **Overview / F** retain your chosen view; **R** restores the engagement default. The compact live panel stays away from the selected actor/target and shows health, role, action and effects; **Advanced details** retains IDs, timing and full profiles. Short You / Ally N / Enemy member labels replace the acronym legend. Circle / split-circle / triangular bases distinguish ownership without color alone. Damaged/selected/critical squads show health; Low marks danger. Gold rings mark the selection and orange its actual living target. Routine hits use existing motion/flash; numbers focus on selected squads, bosses and structures. The approved forest scenery remains.
- Successful nonfinal combat offers three authoritative rewards alongside an expandable factual recap of survivors/losses, Keep damage, sit-outs and earned resources/research. No extra dismissal is needed to choose. Core freezes the last recap before recovery; its factual losses survive a compatible reconnect. Dismissing rewards chooses nothing; **Rewards** reopens the same offers. Wave 20 ends in Victory; defeat offers no draft. **Esc / Menu** opens a framed popup over the current view; **F10 / controller Start** toggles it from any screen. **Continue / Esc** closes it. Opening the menu does not leave or pause the match. **City details** inspects your doctrine, consumed reward receipts, wave and recent feedback. The match-action feedback control shows temporary acceptance and persistent pending, rejected or disconnected status; click it for complete recent messages. There is no permanent Run dashboard or history footer.

**Return to main menu** and **Exit Game** both ask for confirmation describing the consequences. Solo progress is not saved. A host leaving ends the match and disconnects everyone; a guest leaving lets the other players continue and can reconnect only while the same host session remains running. Settings are kept. Cancel or Esc dismisses the confirmation and restores focus without leaving. The match continues while a menu or confirmation is open.

Only one details or rewards surface opens at a time. Esc closes nested Settings, then details/rewards/live inspection, restoring native keyboard focus; otherwise it opens the game menu. Modal shades and native panels consume their input. Disabled actions explain their resource or authority restrictions. Unresolved command errors stay beside match actions; routine accepted results stay in City details and brief factual feedback. Reconnect retains the private credential/session workflow.

`test-gameplay-ui` runs actual native Controls, world picking, modal/focus/scroll
checks, and a separate host/guest game through seven paid cooperative waves. The
new integration fact also belongs to `mise run test` and the existing CI process
suite. `capture-gameplay-ui` captures native panels at 1280×720 and 1920×1080, plus
an actual remote client's read-only allied tree. PNGs are under
`artifacts/captures/gameplay/` (large views under `1920/`).

[GameplayScenarios.Checkpoints](../../src/Game.Shared/GameplayScenarios.cs) owns
the supported default-rule checkpoint names, including the boss forecasts.
They replay paid commands and bounded combat steps; no state is injected. The
`forecast` checkpoint includes partial food funding and a purchased tower. For example:

```sh
mise run play -- --checkpoint army
mise run scenario victory artifacts/scenarios/victory.json
```

The [preparation review](PREPARATION_REVIEW.md) records the completed local
[#3](https://github.com/seraph1nia/common-watch-v2/issues/3) pass in
[#13](https://github.com/seraph1nia/common-watch-v2/issues/13), including observed
solo/cooperative actions, empty stages and waiting. It preserves all three
settlements. Optional human reviews can record actions taken in each preparation,
time spent preparing, time waiting after Ready, and whether a stage felt empty
over the first three waves. Ask players to name the next transition, expected
army/sit-outs and one sensible adjustment before advancing. No human pacing or
balance finding is established by the scripted campaign. A shorter rhythm would
need a separate decision covering purchases between incomes, research, recovery
and final Food payment; one triple-income payment is not an equivalent baseline.

There are no campaign disk saves, new mechanics, replacement world artwork, host migration or
matchmaking. Linux automation and cross-builds do not qualify live Steam accounts,
Windows/macOS runtime, physical GPU or real audio hardware. The previously observed
intermittent native .NET/JIT crash risk remains unresolved.

The [visual army/deployment review](ARMY_DEPLOYMENT_REVIEW.md) records issue #5
interactions, projection boundaries and local native/network evidence.

The optional top-band **Guide** names a current opening objective and links to
production, recruitment or homes during planning. It starts collapsed; expand it for instructions, then collapse it again freely; that preference lasts across sessions in the current app.
Doctrine cards lead with playstyle and expose canonical numbers through **Full
effects & stock**. [Opening review](OPENING_REVIEW.md) documents six paid paths,
resource constraints and actual first-three-wave results without changing rules.

Battle rewards are immediate supplies or recovery. Three cards state the exact
quoted resource gain, or Keep repair and total healing across eligible living
soldiers, including storage. Full-health recovery is omitted; there is no
resurrection or Research-point reward. Closing/reopening never rerolls. Choose
one, wait for authority, then receive one factual receipt and return to planning
or compact team waiting. No extra Claim/Continue action. Resource-cap reductions
are stated in optional detail. Ordinary shipments can recur across waves and do
not create permanent modifiers. Final Victory and eliminated cities receive no
new draft. Worldtree remains the long-term research route, with current targets
and before/after purchase values. City details distinguishes purchased
technologies from consumed reward history. See the
[reward contract and economy comparison](../refinement-ii/21_IMMEDIATE_REWARDS.md).

**Compare recovery / replacement** in a soldier's inspector shows current combat
profiles, exact next-production recovery, current recruit prices and the separate
trainer-upgrade quote. Equipment stays fixed for existing soldiers. Town Hall
storage uses individual places; stored soldiers never fight but still need Food
funding for future recovery. **Review retirement** reveals a separate permanent,
no-refund confirmation; **Keep soldier** cancels without a command. Replacement
remains a separate paid recruit with a new identity and zero service history.

Preparation forecasts name the current tactical problem and enemy equipment.
Hover for the full brief, or open **City details** for the campaign schedule,
actual boss HP/primary profile and clear rewards. Gear checks, mixed pressure and
boss duels ask different questions; the suggestions never submit a command.
Waves 10/20 use larger crowned figures, gold health emphasis and tick-synchronized
anticipation/defeat cues. Bosses retain their original hit/occupancy rules and
single-member squads; secondary attacks have no extra member to hit in them.

Press **Y** during uncovered planning to toggle Ready / Unready for your city. Key repeat, pending commands and pause cannot advance another stage. Final Ready pays income and Food and starts combat immediately; it does not open another recruitment window. Ordinary play keeps three explicit preparations.

Production lists Wood income first and shows per-producer next-Ready output beside the real cost. Establish that income before exhausting Wood; Merchant’s smaller Wood allocation makes early production order matter. The optional Guide offers one context action, and a trainer prompt opens Military directly. It never buys or submits Ready. Zero-Wood recovery remains an explicit paid safety valve.
